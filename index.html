06
Gamification Engine

Mechanics designed for momentum

  • 10 questions per mission creates a manageable practice loop.
  • 3 lives increases tension without over-punishing learners.
  • Timers: 25s / 20s / 15s by difficulty encourage pace and recall.
  • Points: 100 / 150 / 200 reward progression into harder content.
  • Streak system supports emotional reward and repeat usage.
